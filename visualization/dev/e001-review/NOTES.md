## Third spot-comment round

The eight comments supplied on November 17, 2026 are archived in `spot-comments-round-3-archived.json`; the active E-001 comment file was cleared after this pass. Two chapter buttons share the full selector width; the buttons and transition overlay show complete dates, and the playbar shows a date and time. Older comments without this field are treated as specific to the episode where they were made. The field records review intent for present and future episodes; it does not itself change a visualization.
